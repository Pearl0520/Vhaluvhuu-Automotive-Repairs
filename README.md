# Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs Website

## About the Website

This website was created for **Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs** to give the business a professional online presence. I designed the website to make it easier for customers to find information about the business, see the services offered and get in contact with the business when they need assistance with their vehicles.

The design was based around the automotive industry and the Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs branding. I used a simple layout so that visitors can find the information they need without having to search through too many pages.

## What You Can Find on the Website

The website has the following main pages:

* **Home** – Welcomes visitors and gives them a quick introduction to Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs. It also highlights the words **Reliable, Professional and Trusted** and includes a call-to-action for booking a service.

* **About Us** – Gives visitors more information about the business, its background and what it stands for.

* **Services** – Shows the different automotive repair and maintenance services available and gives customers an idea of what the business can help them with.

* **Enquiry / Book a Service** – Gives customers a way to send an enquiry and provide information about the vehicle and the service they need.

* **Contact Us** – Provides the business contact information and location. A Google Maps section is also included to help customers find the business.

## Design and Colours

I used the colours **red, black and white** throughout the website because they match the Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs logo and give the website a strong automotive look.

The website uses the tagline:

**Reliable | Professional | Trusted**

I also tried to keep the design clean and easy to understand. The navigation menu allows users to move between the pages easily, while the buttons and call-to-actions guide customers towards making an enquiry or booking a service.

## Technologies Used

The website was created using:

* **HTML** – Used to create the different webpages and their content.
* **CSS** – Used to style the website, including the colours, fonts, layout and spacing.
* **Google Maps** – Used to show the location of the business.
* **GitHub** – Used to store the website files and manage the project.
* **GitHub Pages** – Used to publish the website online.

## Website Files

The main files used for the website are:

```text
Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs/
│
├── index.html
├── about.html
├── services.html
├── inquiry.html
├── contact.html
├── style.css
└── images/
```

The `index.html` file is the homepage of the website, while the other HTML files contain the different sections of the website. The `style.css` file controls the overall appearance of the website.

## Purpose of the Website

The main goal of this website is to help Vhaluvhuu Automotive Repairs reach customers online.

Through the website, customers can:

* Find out more about the business.
* View the available automotive services.
* Make an enquiry.
* Request a service.
* Find the business contact details.
* Find the business location.

## My Approach

When creating the website, I focused on making it **simple, professional and easy to use**. I wanted the website to represent the type of service customers would expect from an automotive repair business.

I also made sure that the pages followed a consistent design so that the website feels like one complete website rather than separate pages.
